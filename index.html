About this prototype. Created independently as a service design / consumer behaviour university concept. Not affiliated with Oatly. Product images belong to Oatly and are fetched from Oatly's public image service when online. The guided chat is rule-based, not a live generative AI system. Speech recognition needs a supported browser and microphone permission. The browser may send audio to its speech-recognition provider, but this website does not upload chat conversations. The prototype does not collect customer feedback or send it to us. Chat messages and preferences exist only in browser page memory and are erased when reloaded. This website does not upload chats to GitHub or use a database. The speech provider controls its own data-handling practices. Where to Buy provides retailer and store-search links only; no product stock, price, or local assortment is verified. Optional location is used only for retailer search links and is not stored by this page. No price or retailer stock claims are made. Data checked October 2026. Always verify current labels and product availability.
